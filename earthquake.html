Understanding Earthquakes

Learn about earthquake causes, effects, and essential safety measures to protect yourself and your community

What is an Earthquake?

An earthquake is the shaking of the Earth's surface caused by a sudden release of energy in the Earth's crust. This creates seismic waves that travel through the ground, causing the ground to shake and move.

Primary Cause

The Earth's outer shell is divided into several large plates that slowly move over the mantle. When these tectonic plates collide, slide past each other, or pull apart, they create stress. When this stress is suddenly released, it causes an earthquake.

Signs & Effects of Earthquakes

Ground Shaking

The ground moves suddenly, causing buildings and objects to shake.

Aftershocks

Smaller quakes that happen minutes, hours, or even days after the main earthquake.

Ground Cracks

The surface of the Earth may split or crack.

Earthquake Safety Guide

BEFORE AN EARTHQUAKE

  1. Secure heavy furniture and appliances.
  2. Identify safe spots in each room.
  3. Prepare an emergency kit.

BEFORE AN EARTHQUAKE

  1. Create a family communication plan.
  2. Learn how to turn off utilities.
  3. Practice earthquake drills.
  4. Store documents in waterproof container.

DURING AN EARTHQUAKE

  1. Drop to your hands and knees.
  2. Cover your head and neck.
  3. Hold on until shaking stops.
  4. Stay away from windows.

DURING AN EARTHQUAKE

  1. Move to an open area outdoors.
  2. If in a vehicle, pull over safely.
  3. Do not use elevators.

AFTER AN EARTHQUAKE

  1. Check injuries.
  2. Move to open space.
  3. Avoid damaged structures.
  4. Do not use elevators.

AFTER AN EARTHQUAKE

  1. Avoid debris.
  2. Expect aftershocks.
  3. Follow emergency alerts.
