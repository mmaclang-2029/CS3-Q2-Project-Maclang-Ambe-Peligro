Our Purpose

The Soteria Community Solutions Navigator was created with a clear purpose

To provide simple, factual, and accessible information about disasters.
To promote disaster awareness, preparedness, and community safety.
To empower students, families, and local communities.
Our goal? To make safety knowledge clear, reliable, and easy for everyone to use.
With the recent flooding, earthquakes, and other disasters, we created this website to help fellow Filipinos stay informed, prepared, and safe. It provides quick access to important updates, safety tips, and emergency guides. Bangon gihapon Sugboanon! Bahalag bun-og na kaau!
