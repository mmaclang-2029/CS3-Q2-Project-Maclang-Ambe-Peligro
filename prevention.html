Why Prevention Matters

Reducing Damage

Preventive measures greatly reduce property, infrastructure, and economic damage from natural disasters, making them far more cost-effective than recovery efforts.

Protecting Lives

The main goal of disaster prevention is to save lives. With proper planning, education, and stronger infrastructure, communities can greatly reduce casualties and injuries during disasters.

Community-Level Preparedness

Clean Drainage Systems

Regular maintenance and cleaning of drainage systems, canals, and waterways prevent flooding by ensuring proper water flow during heavy rainfall.

Tree Planting Programs

Trees and vegetation help prevent soil erosion, absorb excess water, and provide natural barriers against strong winds and landslides.

Disaster Drills

Regular disaster drills and simulations prepare communities to respond quickly and effectively when real emergencies occur.

Home-Level Preparedness

Strengthening House Structures

• Reinforce roof connections and braces
• Install storm shutters or impact-resistant windows
• Secure heavy furniture
• Check for wall and foundation cracks
• Use durable materials during construction and repairs

Preparing Emergency Kits

• Water (1 gallon per person / day)
• Non-perishable food for 3 days
• First aid kit and medicines
• Flashlight, batteries
• Powered radio
• Plastic sheeting
• Sanitation supplies

Storing Important Documents Safely

• Use waterproof and fireproof containers
• Store ID copies and records
• Keep digital copies saved in cloud services
• Keep legal and insurance information accessible
